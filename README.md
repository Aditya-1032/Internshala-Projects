# Social Media Analytics for Strategic Branding

**Internshala Data Science Training Project | Microsoft Excel**

## 1. Project Overview

This project, *Social Media Analytics for Strategic Branding*, was completed as part of my Data Science training with Internshala Trainings.

The project is based on a business scenario in which I take the role of a Data Analyst at PepsiCo, a global food and beverage company with a presence across social media platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube.

As digital marketing activities expand across multiple platforms, understanding content performance, audience engagement, advertising efficiency, and campaign effectiveness becomes increasingly important.

The objective of this project was to develop an Excel-based social media intelligence solution to analyze marketing performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decisions.

## 2. Business Problem

The marketing team needs a consolidated view of social media performance to answer questions such as:

- Which content types generate the highest engagement?
- Which social media platforms perform best?
- How effectively is the advertising budget being utilized?
- Which hashtags and campaigns perform well?
- What drives follower growth and audience retention?
- How can social media marketing strategies be improved using data?

This project addresses these questions through data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and actionable recommendations.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare social media marketing data for analysis.
- Calculate and compare engagement metrics across platforms.
- Identify high-performing posts, hashtags, and content types.
- Evaluate campaign performance and engagement uplift.
- Analyze follower growth and audience retention trends.
- Explore the relationship between advertising expenditure and follower growth.
- Develop data-driven recommendations for social media marketing strategy.

## 4. Dataset Description

The project uses the PepsiCo Marketing Dataset provided with the Internshala assignment. It consists of three datasets.

### A. Posts Dataset

Contains individual social media post information, including:

- Post ID, platform, and publication date
- Content type and caption
- Likes, shares, and comments
- Impressions, reach, and clicks
- Hashtags used and campaign name

### B. Engagement Summary Dataset

Contains weekly platform-level performance information, including:

- Week start date and platform
- New followers and unfollows
- Total followers
- Engagement rate
- Advertising spend

### C. Campaign Metadata Dataset

Contains high-level information about marketing campaigns, including:

- Campaign name and campaign dates
- Campaign objective
- Total allocated budget
- Target platforms
- Primary hashtags

## 5. Project Tasks and Scope

The assignment consists of seven tasks covering data preparation, social media analytics, campaign evaluation, and communication of findings.

### Task 1: Data Preprocessing and Cleaning

**Objective:** Prepare the datasets for reliable analysis.

Tasks performed or specified in the assignment:

- Remove duplicate post entries.
- Standardize date formats and platform names.
- Ensure numeric columns, including likes, reach, impressions, and advertising spend, have appropriate formatting.
- Split hashtags into individual tags.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Excel Power Query.

### Task 2: Engagement Analysis

**Objective:** Evaluate engagement performance across posts and platforms.

- Calculate average engagement rates by platform.
- Identify the top 10 posts with the highest engagement.
- Create PivotTables showing total likes, shares, and comments by content type and platform.
- Calculate average clicks per post by hashtag.
- Rank hashtags using formula-based calculations.

The assignment defines engagement rate as:

`Engagement Rate = (Likes + Shares + Comments) / Impressions`

**Tools:** Excel formulas, PivotTables, ranking formulas.

### Task 3: Social Media Platform Analysis

**Objective:** Compare platform performance and advertising efficiency.

- Identify the platform generating the highest engagement.
- Compare follower growth rates across platforms.
- Visualize engagement in relation to advertising spend across platforms.
- Recommend whether to focus on one or two core platforms or continue a multi-platform strategy, supported by the analysis.

**Tools:** Excel formulas, PivotTables, charts.

### Task 4: Hashtag and Content Strategy

**Objective:** Understand which hashtags and content formats perform best.

- Identify the most frequently used hashtags.
- Calculate average likes and comments for posts containing each hashtag.
- Compare the performance of videos, images, and carousels across platforms.
- Recommend which content types should be prioritized on each platform.

**Tools:** Excel formulas, PivotTables, comparative analysis.

### Task 5: Campaign Effectiveness

**Objective:** Evaluate campaign outcomes against marketing objectives and budget.

- Calculate total and average impressions, likes, and clicks for each campaign.
- Calculate engagement uplift during campaigns compared with the period before campaigns.
- Evaluate engagement performance relative to campaign budgets.
- Identify campaigns with the highest engagement return relative to budget.
- Identify campaigns with the strongest follower growth impact.

**Tools:** Excel formulas, campaign-level analysis, comparative metrics.

### Task 6: Follower Retention and Audience Loyalty

**Objective:** Analyze audience growth patterns and their relationship with advertising expenditure.

- Visualize weekly follower growth trends by platform using line charts.
- Identify the week with the highest net follower gain.
- Create moving-average charts to smooth follower growth trends.
- Investigate the relationship between advertising spend and follower growth using scatter plots and correlation calculations.

**Tools:** Excel formulas, line charts, scatter plots, moving averages, correlation analysis.

### Task 7: Findings and Actionable Recommendations

**Objective:** Communicate the key findings and translate the analysis into practical recommendations.

The assignment requires a video explanation summarizing the findings and providing data-backed recommendations for:

- Social media marketing strategy
- Campaign planning and effectiveness
- Content performance
- Platform-specific performance and return on investment

**Deliverable:** A video presentation explaining the findings and recommendations in my own words.

## 6. Tools and Techniques
